# mp2-f12: explicitly correlated MP2

`mp2-f12` (exact integrals) and `df-mp2-f12` (density fitting) compute MP2-F12 with
fixed amplitudes (the SP ansatz of Ten-no: geminal amplitudes 1/2 for singlet and 1/4
for triplet pairs; ansatz 3) on top of an RHF reference. The approximations are
Molpro's 3C and 3\*C (approximation C for the B matrix), optionally with NOZ and HY1,
and 3A, 3\*A (approximation A, as Turbomole's fixed-A); formulas in
[Theory](../theory.md).

- `mp2-f12`: exact integrals for all F12 operators and the Fock matrices in the RI
  basis (orbital basis + CABS). The indices of the occupied orbitals run over the
  orbital basis only, the others over the RI or orbital basis as each operator needs;
  the cost still grows with the fourth power of the basis, so this is for small
  molecules (water/aug-cc-pVTZ: 7 s for the pair integrals), mainly to check DF.
- `df-mp2-f12`: robust density fitting with the correlation fitting basis
  (`c_auxbasis`), Fock matrices with the JK fitting basis. The DF error of the F12
  correction is of the order of 1e-5 Eh (water/aug-cc-pVTZ 1e-5, aug-cc-pVDZ 5e-5, mostly
  from the Fock matrices in the RI basis).


```yaml
systems:
  water: {coordinates: h2o.xyz, basis: aug-cc-pVTZ}
tasks:
  - {system: water, task: df-rhf}
  - task: df-mp2-f12
    approximation: 3c                  # default; 3*c, 3a, 3*a, or a list, e.g. [3a, 3c]
    gamma: 1.0                         # geminal exponent (default)
    pair_energies: true
```

## Output

- The orbital spaces as for [mp2](mp2.md) (frozen, occupied inner core / outer core /
  valence, virtual, deleted; also in the results as `frozen_per_irrep`,
  `occupied_per_irrep`, `virtual_per_irrep`, `deleted_per_irrep`).
- The geminal: its Gaussian fit (STG-6G by default; exponents and coefficients printed),
  `geminal: exact` for the Slater function, or the given `geminal_exponents` and
  `geminal_coefficients` (e.g. Molpro's values for an exact comparison).
- The CABS and the CABS singles (see below), the fitting basis and the storage of the
  3-index integrals (`integrals: memory | disk | auto`, as for mp2).
- A table with the singlet, triplet and total MP2 energy and the F12 corrections of the
  computed approximations (3\*C and 3C with the NOZ/HY1 flags, 3\*A and 3A); then the
  reference energy (SCF + CABS singles), the MP2 and F12 parts of the main approximation
  and the MP2-F12 total energy. With `pair_energies: true` the singlet and triplet pair energies of MP2,
  of the F12 correction (selected approximation) and their sum (pairs $i \ge j$ of the
  correlated occupied orbitals in energy order, labels $n.k$ as in the SCF output; $ij$ and
  $ji$ together). The results always contain them as
  `pair_energies` (lists `i`, `j` (labels), `mp2_singlet`, `mp2_triplet`, `f12_singlet`,
  `f12_triplet`; triplet 0 for $i = j$).
- If core orbitals are correlated (e.g. `frozen: off`): the MP2, F12 and MP2-F12
  correlation energies split by pairs into core, core-valence and valence parts as for
  [mp2](mp2.md) (results `e_mp2_core`, `e_mp2_core_valence`, `e_mp2_valence`, `e_f12_core`,
  ...; core orbitals marked `*` in the pair energies).

## Approximations

`approximation` is one value or a list. Each value computes its pair of approximations:
`3c` or `3*c` gives 3C and 3\*C, `3a` or `3*a` gives 3A and 3\*A; a list gives all named
pairs. The first value is the main result (`e_f12`, singlet and triplet parts, pair
energies, core/valence parts); the results hold `e_f12_3c`, `e_f12_3sc`, `e_f12_3a`,
`e_f12_3sa` of the computed approximations.

| value | Molpro | B matrix and coupling |
|---|---|---|
| `3c` | `3C(FIX)` | all terms of approximation C, F C term in B, coupling of the conventional amplitudes |
| `3*c` | `3*C(FIX)` | without the F C term and the coupling |
| `noz: true` | `NOZ` | without the Z terms (occupied–CABS Fock elements; GBC in B) |
| `hy1: true` | `HY1` | without the exchange terms of the double RI expansion |
| `3a` | (Turbomole fixed-A) | B of approximation A (Hartree commutator only), no X term, F C term and coupling as 3C |
| `3*a` | | as 3a, without the F C term and the coupling |

Approximation A needs no F² integrals but the F12 integrals of the Hartree-transformed
occupied orbitals $\sum_{p'} |p'\rangle (h+J)_{p'k}$ (RI projection; see
[Theory](../theory.md)): with density fitting two 3-index arrays of the size of the f12
ones instead of one (F²); with exact integrals an integral pass with the occupied indices
over the RI basis instead of the F² pass. NOZ and HY1 apply to approximation C only (an
error with approximation A alone), and `mp2-f12xg-pilot` has no approximation A yet.
Water/aug-cc-pVTZ agrees with Turbomole's fixed-A to 1e-10 Eh (using same geminal fit, CABS
and fitting bases, all-electron CABS singles). Molpro's 3A(FIX) differs by about 5e-5 Eh
(different approximations for $B$ and $C$ intermediates).

Molpro switches NOZ on with HY1 (`3C(FIX,HY1)` is run as `3C(FIX,NOZ,HY1)`); here the two
flags are independent.

For a comparison with Molpro's `df-mp2-f12`: STG-6G with γ = 1 (default), the JK basis as
the CABS (`cabs_auxbasis: aug-cc-pVTZ-JK` for aug-cc-pVTZ; Molpro's default RI basis),
the MP2FIT basis (`aug-cc-pVTZ-C`, default) and `all_electron_cabs: false` with frozen
core. Water/aug-cc-pVTZ then agrees with Molpro to 1e-11 Eh in all variants of approximation C.

## CABS and auxiliary basis sets

- **RI basis**: the orbital basis and the CABS (auxiliary) basis together. The CABS
  orbitals are the auxiliary functions with the orbital basis projected out,
  orthonormalized. Eigenvalues of their overlap below `cabs_threshold` are dropped (as
  linear dependencies). The output lists the number of CABS orbitals, the dropped ones
  and the smallest eigenvalues.
- **Fock matrices** in the RI basis: with exact integrals for `mp2-f12`; with the JK
  fitting basis for `df-mp2-f12`.
- **Auxiliary basis sets** (`jk_auxbasis`, `c_auxbasis`, `cabs_auxbasis`): each is taken
  from, in this order:
  1. the task option;
  2. the one used by an earlier task on the same system;
  3. the system key;
  4. the default from `lib/basis/auxbasis.yaml` (files `<basis>-JK`, `<basis>-C`,
     `<basis>-CABS` are found automatically).

  Steps 1, 3 and 4 work per atom: a mapping per element or atom may name only some
  atoms, the others follow the next step (see
  [Systems](index.md#basis-sets-per-element-and-atom)).

  For aug-cc-pVXZ bases, Molpro uses the JK fitting basis as the RI basis unless told otherwise. For comparisons
  with Molpro, set `cabs_auxbasis` to the JK fitting basis (e.g. aug-cc-pVTZ-JK for
  aug-cc-pVTZ). aug-cc-pVXZ-JK (X = T, Q, 5) are not on the Basis Set Exchange;
  they are generated from cc-pVXZ-JK by `lib/basis/augment_jk.py` and equal Molpro's
  AVXZ/JKFIT (water DF-RHF energy and CABS singles agree to 1e-12 Eh).

## CABS singles

The CABS singles correction is the second-order singles energy from the occupied
orbitals into the virtual and CABS orbitals, with the Fock matrix in the RI basis. It is
added to the SCF energy to give the reference energy.

- `all_electron_cabs: true` (default) includes the frozen core orbitals.
- With `false`, only the correlated occupied orbitals are included. This is what Molpro
  does: with a frozen 1s, the CABS singles of water change by about 1.3e-4 Eh.

## Density fitting and size consistency

`mp2-f12` is size consistent: for He…Ne at 50 Å, all parts are additive to 1e-13 Eh.
`df-mp2-f12` is additive only to about 1e-7 Eh there (aug-cc-pVDZ, def2-universal-JK:
F12 correction 1.9e-7, CABS singles −3.6e-8, MP2 2e-10 Eh). This is inherent to fitting
with the Coulomb metric and not a cutoff: the deviation decays as 1/R, does not depend on
the metric eigenvalue cutoff, the screening or the storage of the 3-index integrals, and
shrinks with larger fitting bases (F12 part: 1.6e-8 with aug-cc-pVTZ-C, 2.3e-9 with
aug-cc-pVQZ-C as JK and correlation fitting basis).

The fit is global: the metric (P|Q) couples fitting functions on distant fragments
through their charges (about q_P q_Q / R). A fitted quantity with Coulomb-metric fitting
has an error of second order, the Coulomb interaction of two fitting errors, e.g.
(δ_μν|δρ) for the Coulomb matrix element J_μν. With the fragments apart, the errors on
different fragments interact through their charges, which gives a term of the order
δq_μν δq_ρ / R. In the SCF energy both errors are small (density fitting of the orbital
basis), so the SCF energy stays additive to 1e-13 Eh. In the RI basis, the products of
occupied and CABS functions are fitted poorly by a JK basis. Their charge error is large,
so the occupied–CABS Fock elements, the CABS singles and, through the same mechanism in
the robust fitting of the geminal integrals (which also gives the inter-fragment pairs
an F12 contribution), the F12 correction pick up 1/R terms. Of the CABS singles
deviation, the Coulomb matrix gives almost all (−3.8e-8 Eh), exchange 2e-9 Eh. Molpro
fits the same way and agrees with `df-mp2-f12` to 1e-11 Eh (water), so it shows the
same behaviour.
